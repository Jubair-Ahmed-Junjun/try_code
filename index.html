Biography

I am a cheerful, optimistic, and highly self-motivated person, with a strong commitment to learning, professional growth, and scientific work. I am hardworking, persistent in the face of challenges, and confident in taking responsibility and leadership.

Currently, I serve as Lab In-Charge (Biochemist) at the Institute of Applied Health Sciences Hospital – USTC and as a Research Assistant at CUET. My current research encompasses in-vivo and in-vitro studies, with particular interests in cancer biology, genetics, immunology, and molecular biology.

I seek to investigate complex biological questions and contribute to evidence-based advances in biomedical science through interdisciplinary research integrating biology, chemistry, and clinical science.
